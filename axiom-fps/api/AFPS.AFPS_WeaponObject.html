True if ANY ammo pool on this weapon is currently mid-reload. Aggregates IsReloading across all pools (e.g. an underbarrel launcher with its own pool). Read by AFPS_WeaponTilt to drive the reloading tilt pose.

CancelReloads()

Cancels any in-progress reload (and pending reload-hold) on every ammo pool on this weapon. Called whenever the weapon stops being the active weapon — switched away, holstered, dropped, or unequipped — so a reload can't keep running in the background (the weapon GameObject stays active on a switch, only its model is hidden) and complete on a weapon the player isn't holding.

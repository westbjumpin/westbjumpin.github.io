Struct ScreenEffectRequest

Describes one full-screen status overlay: the image to fade in (a sprite/material such as a frost vignette or fire border, or a flat colour), its peak opacity, and the fade timings. Authored on AFPS_ScreenEffectStatusBehaviour and handed to AFPS_StatusScreenEffects.

Class AFPS_SpawnPointRegistry

Scene-side list of respawn points for AFPS_SpawnPointRespawnStrategy. The strategy is a ScriptableObject and so cannot hold scene references — this component is where the actual transforms live, and the strategy asks it for one at respawn time.

Drop it on an empty GameObject and either fill spawnPoints by hand or parent the points under it and let collectChildrenOnAwake gather them.

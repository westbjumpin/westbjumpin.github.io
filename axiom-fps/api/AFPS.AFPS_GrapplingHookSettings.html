Class AFPS_GrapplingHookSettings

Grappling hook preset: every tuning value for GrapplingHook — attach rules, reel and momentum, pendulum tension, swing control, movable-anchor mass split, and the auto-detach conditions.

Swap the asset to swap the whole feel of the grapple. Scene-bound references (the rope LineRenderer) and the SFX containers stay on the component, since those are per-instance rather than per-preset.

Struct AFPS_MovementAnimationSnapshot

One frame's answer to "what is the player doing?", in the form every animation system in the asset needs it. Sampled once per consumer per frame and passed around by value.

Every field is derived, never authored: the weapon and arms animators must agree on what counts as swimming or climbing, and the only way to guarantee that is for neither of them to decide.

IsClimbing

Player is climbing a wall, or is on a ladder and actually moving UP it. Ladders reuse the climb animation, and the upward test is deliberate: at or below the threshold the player is hanging idle (or sliding down) and the climbing reach reads wrong. This is exactly the condition the arms controller has always used — isClimbing || climbingLadder.

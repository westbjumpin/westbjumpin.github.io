Class AFPS_DeathController

Owns the window between dying and respawning. Add it to the player and Die() stops respawning instantly and hands the death over here instead; remove it and the asset reverts to its original instant-checkpoint behaviour. Nothing else has to change either way — the presence of this component is the switch.

The sequence itself is described entirely by the AFPS_DeathSettings asset in settings: timing, camera mode, respawn strategy, screen text, and policy. Swapping that one asset is how a flat "YOU DIED" overlay becomes a killcam.

Runs at a late execution order so its camera drive lands after AFPS_Camera has had its turn, and times everything on the unscaled clock so death-screen countdowns survive time dilation.

public class AFPS_DeathController : MonoBehaviour, IDeathSequence
Remarks

Setup. Add this component to the player root (the GameObject carrying AFPS_Health) — do it on the player prefab, not a scene instance, since the references it needs live inside the prefab. Adding it in the editor runs AFPS.AFPS_DeathController.Reset(), which resolves health, movement, cameraController, weaponSystem, uiManager, and bodyHandler automatically; anything left unresolved is resolved again in AFPS.AFPS_DeathController.Awake(). Then assign a settings preset from Presets/Death and press play. That alone gives a working death sequence — camera, timing, and respawn — with no on-screen text.

On-screen text (optional). Assign a deathScreen — either AFPS_DeathScreenUIToolkit on a UIDocument sourcing UI Toolkit/DeathScreen/DeathScreen.uxml with the shipped DeathScreenPanelSettings asset (already at a sort order above the HUD and below the menus), or AFPS_DeathScreenUGUI for the Canvas HUD path. Keep the screen's GameObject active — it hides itself by fading its own root, so disabling the GameObject only stops it from working. Leaving the slot empty is supported: the death plays as a pure camera sequence. Put the HUD objects that should vanish while dead (the crosshair/ammo document or canvas) into hideWhileDead.

Leaving first person (optional). Any AFPS_DeathCameraMode that moves the view off the head (deathcam, freezecam, orbit, follow-body) needs something to look at, or it frames an empty first-person capsule. Add an AFPS_DeathBodyHandler to the player and pick a body mode (hide the body, or spawn a corpse prefab).

Spawn-point respawns (optional). A AFPS_SpawnPointRespawnStrategy needs an AFPS_SpawnPointRegistry somewhere in the scene; the checkpoint strategy uses the existing AFPS_CheckpointManager and needs nothing extra.

Two things silently defeat a death screen: infiniteHealth left on (the player can't die), and a checkpoint respawn with no current checkpoint assigned. The inspector's "Debug: Kill Player" button previews the whole sequence in play mode.

Respawn()

Puts the player back into play: runs the respawn strategy, restores health, camera, body, input, and HUD, then clears the death latch on AFPS_Health. Safe to call from anywhere — it is how Manual sequences are ended — and no-ops when no death is in progress.

Class AFPS_SpawnPointRespawnStrategy

Respawns at one of the points registered with AFPS_SpawnPointRegistry. Position and facing both come from the point, and the player is healed by the controller (this strategy does not restore HP itself), so it drops straight into arena-style games with no checkpoint anywhere.

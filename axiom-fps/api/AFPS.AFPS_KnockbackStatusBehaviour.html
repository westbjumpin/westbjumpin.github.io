Class AFPS_KnockbackStatusBehaviour

Status behaviour that knocks the player back and strips their control until they land again — the classic "launched and helpless" effect. On apply it launches the player's Rigidbody away from whoever applied the effect (plus an upward kick) and holds a control lock that releases itself the moment the player touches the ground; the effect's own duration is a safety cap that also releases the lock if the player somehow never lands.

Player-only (drives the player's Rigidbody + control lock). Direction comes from the effect's instigator (instigator) — so a rocket, a punch, or a barrel blast all shove the player away from the source.

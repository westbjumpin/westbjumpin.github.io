Class AFPS_ContainerInventory

A lootable world container — chest, crate, locker, dead body, wall stash. Interacting binds its contents to the inventory's secondary grid, resized to the size declared here, so the player loots through the same drag / rotate / stack interactions they already use on their own inventory.

Contents are live AFPS_ItemObject instances. While closed they are deactivated and parked under this GameObject, keeping their stack sizes, rotation, nested storage and the world object they were picked up from; opening re-seats them in the cells they were left in. Authored loot (startingContents, lootTable) stays as lightweight descriptors until first open.

Setup: put this on a world object with a collider on the interaction layer, and give the player an AFPS_ContainerUIController.

AdoptItem(AFPS_ItemObject)

Takes ownership of an EXISTING item object, preserving its identity: stack size, rotation, nested storage, and the world object it was originally picked up from all survive. This is what a death drop needs — AddContents(AFPS_Item, int) would rebuild the item from its asset and lose all of that.

The item is laid out afresh on the next open rather than at a remembered cell, since it never occupied one in this container.

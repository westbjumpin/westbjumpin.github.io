Class AFPS_DashSettings

Dash preset: every tuning value for Dash — direction mode and force, the pre-dash velocity reset, the momentum the dash keeps afterwards, and the optional charge-up windup.

Swap the asset to swap the whole feel of the dash. Only the SFX containers stay on the component, since those are per-instance rather than per-preset.

floatDisableTime

Seconds the player's hover float PID is suppressed after a FromCamera or Upwards dash, via SetFloatTimer(float).

Both of those modes can launch the player off the ground, and the PID's job is to hold a fixed hover height — left running it pushes back against the launch and clips the dash short. Needs to be long enough for the player to clear the float ray, not so long that landing feels like a drop.

momentumPreserved

How much of the dash's speed survives the seconds after it fires, as the share of the player's drag that is suppressed while the preservation window runs.

The controller's ground/air acceleration only ever adds speed along the movement vector — it never brakes — so the one thing eating a dash is UnityEngine.Rigidbody.linearDamping, which AFPS_Movement rewrites from its own state every frame. 0 hands drag straight back and the dash bleeds off at the authored ground/air rate (the original behaviour); 1 removes drag entirely for momentumPreserveTime, so the player keeps every bit of the dash and carries it into the next jump or slide.

Declaration
[BoxGroup("Momentum Preservation")]
[Range(0, 1)]
[Tooltip("Share of the player's drag suppressed after a dash. 0 = dash speed bleeds off at the normal rate, 1 = the dash keeps all of its speed for the window below")]
public float momentumPreserved

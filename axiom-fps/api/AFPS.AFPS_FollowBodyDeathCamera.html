Class AFPS_FollowBodyDeathCamera

Third-person camera that tracks the body — the corpse prefab or ragdoll produced by AFPS_DeathBodyHandler, or the player transform when neither exists. Pairs with a ragdoll: the camera hangs back at a fixed offset while the body tumbles, so the fall is readable.

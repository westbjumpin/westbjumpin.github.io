Class StatusEffectResistance

Per-status-effect resistance on a target: optionally blocks the effect from being applied, and scales how long it lasts, its per-tick HP impact, and the strength of its non-HP behaviours (slow, screen overlay, knockback, …). Matched by the effect's source asset in GiveNewStatusEffect(AFPS_StatusEffect, GameObject); effects with no matching entry apply at full strength.

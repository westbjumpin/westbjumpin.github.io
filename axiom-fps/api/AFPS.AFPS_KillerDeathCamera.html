Class AFPS_KillerDeathCamera

Killcam: frames whoever landed the killing blow, resolved from the latched LastDamage instigator. The camera sits over the victim's body and looks down the line to the killer, keeping them framed as they move.

Deaths with nothing to blame — a fall, drowning, a status effect that outlived its caster — have no killer to point at. Set fallbackWhenNoKiller so those still play something deliberate instead of holding a shot of empty air.
